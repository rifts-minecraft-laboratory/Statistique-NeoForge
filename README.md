# Welcome to *Statistiques*

> *Statistiques*  
> From the French word *statistique* (statistic), itself derived from the German *Statistik*, coined in 1749 by economist Gottfried Achenwall. The term ultimately traces back to the Italian *statista*, meaning "statesman".  
> Originally, it referred to the knowledge required to govern a state, but it quickly evolved to describe the collection and analysis of measurable data across many fields.

Today, statistics are everywhere—and incredibly useful. That’s exactly what *Statistiques* brings into Minecraft.

*Statistiques* is a mod that tracks detailed, equipment-based data across your entire gear setup. Weapons, tools, and armor all come with their own dedicated statistics, giving you deep insight into how you actually use them. From blocks mined and damage dealt to arrows fired, missed shots, and beyond—every action is recorded and turned into meaningful data.

### Analyze Your Gameplay
This mod is built for players who love digging into the numbers. Whether you want to optimize your resource gathering, analyze your combat efficiency, or simply track the wear and tear of your favorite tools, *Statistiques* provides the comprehensive data you need. Stop guessing about your progress and start measuring it—let the raw data reveal exactly how you play and how your equipment performs over time.
